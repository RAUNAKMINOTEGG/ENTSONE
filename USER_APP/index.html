ABOUT ENTSONE

ENTSONE

ENTSONE is a competitive gaming tournament platform built to bring players and esports events together in one place.

Players can discover games, join tournaments, manage teams and follow match progress from their profile.

Tournament organizers can manage events, entry fees, prize pools, rooms, results and player registrations through the admin system.

Live tournament status, room credentials, leaderboards and notifications are connected to the platform backend.

ENTSONE is designed with mobile-first access, secure user authorization and organized competitive play in mind.

Payment remains DEMO-only. Account verification uses the configured real SMS OTP provider in production, while tournament and registration data are stored on the real server database.

The goal is to build a reliable, fair and growing esports community where players can compete, improve and enjoy organized gaming.

Platform Policies

Terms of Service · Privacy Policy · Tournament Rules · Contact & Support

TERMS

Terms of Service

ENTSONE provides a tournament-management platform. Users must provide accurate registration information, follow tournament rules, and use the platform lawfully.

Tournament entry and prize information shown in this demo release are informational. Payment processing is DEMO ONLY and no real money is collected through this release.

Organizers may modify, postpone or cancel events according to published rules. Abuse, cheating, impersonation and attempts to bypass authorization may result in account or tournament restrictions.

PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

ENTSONE stores account, tournament registration, team and result information needed to operate the service. Authentication sessions are protected server-side.

Account verification uses real SMS OTP when the production provider is configured. Sensitive room credentials are returned only to authenticated users who are registered for the tournament.

Data may be retained for tournament history, support, security and administration. Do not submit unnecessary sensitive personal information.

RULES

Tournament Rules

Join only with valid account information. Follow the game-specific rules, check-in requirements, match timing and organizer instructions.

Results may remain pending until admin verification. Verified results are used for leaderboard and player statistics.

Cheating, collusion, abuse, fake registrations and unauthorized access to room information are prohibited.
